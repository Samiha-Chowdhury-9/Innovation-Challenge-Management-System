We propose the development of the Innovation Challenge Management System (ICMS). It is a relational database system for managing innovation competitions and hackathons. The system will support the full competition process. This includes participant registration, team formation, submissions, judging, mentorship, and prize distribution. All important information will be stored in one organized database.

**Problem Statement**

Innovation challenges are often managed using spreadsheets, emails, documents, and manual score sheets. This can work for small events. However, it becomes difficult when many teams, judges, mentors, and rounds are involved. Information may be duplicated, missing, or incorrect. Organizers may also struggle to track submission deadlines, evaluation progress, and final scores. Some submissions may not receive all the required evaluations. Prize distribution may also become slow and confusing. A single and reliable system is needed to manage the whole process.

**Proposed Solution**

ICMS will store all challenge-related information in one database. Organizers will be able to create challenges and divide them into different rounds. These rounds may include Ideation, Prototype, and Final Pitch. Participants will be able to register and form teams. Each team will join a challenge and submit its work for the required rounds. The system will record submission details, dates, versions, and current status. Judges will evaluate submissions using specific scoring criteria. They will provide scores and comments. The system will also track whether all required evaluations have been completed. Mentors will be able to guide teams through mentorship sessions. The date and notes of each session will be recorded. Sponsors, prizes, and winning teams will also be managed by the system. Important rules will be handled automatically. The system will prevent submissions after a challenge is closed. It will block late team registrations. It will check whether mentorship sessions are held within the challenge period. It will also update submission status and assign prizes to top-performing teams.

This project will cover the complete database development process. It will include the entity-relationship diagram, relational schema, and normalization up to Third Normal Form. The required tables and constraints will also be created. Sample data will be added to test the system. SQL queries will be used to retrieve and analyze information. PL/SQL programs will also be developed. These will include conditional statements, cursors, functions, procedures, and triggers. They will help apply the real-life rules of the system.

The final outcome will be a working database system for managing an innovation challenge from beginning to end.
