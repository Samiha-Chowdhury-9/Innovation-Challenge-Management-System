In an Innovation Challenge Management System, a challenge is identified by a challenge ID. The system also stores the challenge title, theme, status, start date and end date. The duration of a challenge is calculated from its start date and end date. A challenge may have many rounds. Every round must be associated with a challenge via HasRound. A round is identified by a round ID. The system also stores the round number and round name. 

A participant is identified by a participant ID. The system also stores the participant’s name, email address and affiliation. A participant may have multiple phone numbers. Participants may form teams. A participant may belong to many teams and a team may have many participants. A team is identified by a team ID. The system also stores the team name. A challenge may have many registered teams. A team may register for a challenge. A team may make many submissions. A submission may be made by a team. 

A round may contain many submissions. A submission may belong to a round. A submission is identified by a submission ID. The system also stores its title, abstract, status, submitted date and version number. A submission may have many tags and a tag may be associated with many submissions. A tag is identified by a tag ID. The system also stores the tag name. A round may define many criteria. Every criterion must be associated with a round via Defines. A criterion is identified by a criteria ID. The system also stores the criterion name. A submission may undergo many evaluations. An evaluation may be related to a submission. A criterion may be applied in many evaluations. An evaluation may use a criterion. An evaluation is identified by an evaluation ID. The system also stores the score, comments, evaluation time, the person who locked it and the locking time. 

A judge may perform many evaluations. Every evaluation must be associated with a judge via Performs. Mentor and Judge can be generalized as Participant. Participant can be specialized into Mentor and Judge. A mentor has a specialization and a judge has an area of expertise. A mentor may conduct many mentorship sessions. A team may receive many mentorship sessions. The existence of MentorshipSession is totally dependent on its associated Mentor and Team. Every mentorship session must be associated with a mentor via Conducts and a team via Receives. The system stores the session date and notes. The session date is a partial identifier of MentorshipSession. A sponsor is identified by a sponsor ID. The system also stores the sponsor name and contribution amount. 

A sponsor may fund many prizes. Every prize must be associated with a sponsor via Funds. A challenge may offer many prizes. Every prize must be associated with a challenge via Offers. A prize is identified by a prize ID. The system also stores the prize name, amount and rank. A team may win many prizes. Every prize must be associated with a team via Wins. A sponsor is identified by a Sponsor ID. The system also stores the sponsor name and contribution amount. A sponsor may fund many prizes. Every prize must be associated with exactly one sponsor via Funds. A prize is identified by a Prize ID. The system also stores the prize name, amount and rank. A challenge may offer many prizes. Every prize must be associated with exactly one challenge via Offers. A team may win many prizes. Every prize must be associated with exactly one team via Wins.
